Privacy policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

This policy covers the personal Google OAuth utilities Mailhub and IdleForge rclone operated by iamkaku.

Google data access

Mailhub uses Google OAuth to insert mail into the operator's own Gmail account. IdleForge rclone uses Google OAuth to access the operator's own Google Drive. Access occurs only after the Google account owner authenticates and grants consent.

Storage and use

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in private operator-managed infrastructure. They are not stored or published by this GitHub Pages site. Google account data is used only for the personal functions described above.

Sharing

These utilities do not sell Google account data or provide it to third parties. The public availability of this information page does not grant anyone access to the operator's Google accounts or data.

This website

This site contains no advertising or analytics code and does not provide a login or public application service.
