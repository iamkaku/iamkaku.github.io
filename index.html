iamkaku personal OAuth utilities

This page provides the public information required for Google OAuth consent for personal utilities operated by iamkaku.

Applications

Mailhub imports mail from the operator's own source mailboxes into the operator's own Gmail account.

IdleForge rclone lets the operator access the operator's own Google Drive through rclone.

These are personal utilities, not public hosted services. They do not offer accounts or access to third parties. No OAuth credentials, tokens, or Google account data are published on this site.
